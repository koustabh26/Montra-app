# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive.
- Giving and accepting feedback graciously.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.
- Showing empathy toward others.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when an individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report violations privately to the maintainers using GitHub's private reporting channel for this repository (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how it is enabled), or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

> **MAINTAINER ACTION REQUIRED:** add a dedicated, monitored contact method here before publishing. No contact details have been invented in this file.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy respected.
